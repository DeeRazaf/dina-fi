Case study 01 Established DTC brand

Email goes from 17% to 38% of revenue in 5 months.

The starting point

Strong paid acquisition, but email running on autopilot: 17% of revenue, flows built once and never touched again, no campaign calendar.

What I did

  • Rebuilt the core flows: welcome, cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase
  • Engagement-based segmentation and list cleaning for deliverability
  • A campaign calendar run every week, tested and adjusted continuously

The result

Email becomes the account's #1 channel. Attributed share climbs from 17.3% to 37.6% of revenue: €321,987 generated, up from €31,846.

Case study 02 Store at launch stage

From $143 to $41,568 in email revenue, after the core flows.

The starting point

A brand-new store: zero active flows, email at 1.7% of revenue. Everything came from one-off sends and direct traffic. Nothing was automated.

What I did

  • First structured campaigns to activate and retain the list
